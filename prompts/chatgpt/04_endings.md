# 04. 엔딩 4종

게임의 4가지 엔딩 분기. 각 엔딩의 톤은 명확히 구분됨:
A=무지의 평화 / B=막연한 불안 / C=각성 / D=공허(흡수).

---

## ENDING_A — 괜찮은 직장 (무지)

**조건**: 기본 분기. 아무 일도 없었다는 듯 퇴근.

```
A wide cinematic shot of a lone figure in a long-sleeve white clinical uniform walking away from the camera, toward a distant horizon at sunset. The architecture of the underground facility's surface entrance — a low concrete bunker with glass — is visible behind the figure, receding into the background. The sunset is muted, desaturated, almost grey-orange rather than warm. The figure is small in the frame, alone. A sense of hollow peace — calm, not relief. Heavily desaturated colors, 8k photorealistic. Avoid: any face in frame, dramatic dawn colors, any other people.
```

**2회차 변형**:
```
Same lone figure, same path away from the bunker — but in the lower corner of the frame, a single tablet has been dropped on the ground, screen-up, faintly displaying a residual emotion gauge that the walking figure has not noticed and will never look back at. The tablet glows quietly.
```

---

## ENDING_B — 무언가 이상한 (불안)

**조건**: 대상자에게 말을 걸었거나, 고통을 지켜본 분기.

```
A first-person POV shot inside a dimly lit minimalist apartment at night. In the foreground, a single medical tablet lies face-up on a low table, glowing with a faint, ominous blue light in the otherwise dark room. Cold blue moonlight slants through a tall narrow window in the background, casting long deep shadows across the floor. The apartment is sparsely furnished, sterile in its emptiness — almost identical in mood to the facility itself. The atmosphere is oppressive, watching, like the work has followed the protagonist home. 8k photorealistic. Avoid: any people, warm light, comfort.
```

**2회차 변형**:
```
Same dim apartment, same glowing tablet on the table — but the tablet's faint blue glow now reads, just barely visible: "RESIDUAL EMOTION: 84% — DECREASING". The window in the background reflects, faintly, a figure standing where the camera is — though no one should be there.
```

---

## ENDING_C — 거울 (각성)

**조건**: G구역을 엿본 분기.

```
An extreme close-up of a polished elevator interior mirror. Reflected in it is the protagonist's face — unnervingly still, perfectly emotionless, eyes vacant. The reflection is sharp and clear in the center, but the edges of the mirror surface are corrupted by faint glitch artifacts and chromatic aberration, as if the mirror itself is malfunctioning. Cold flickering overhead light from the elevator ceiling, 8k photorealistic. The face must read as the protagonist's, but already not quite human anymore. Avoid: any visible body below the neck, any other person.
```

**2회차 변형**:
```
Same mirror, same vacant reflected face — but now, faintly superimposed across the forehead in clean clinical typography, a digital label reads "SUBJECT #042". The label glitches on and off twice within the same frame, half-buried in the chromatic aberration. The face does not react.
```

---

## ENDING_D — 공허 (흡수)

**조건**: 잔여 감정 게이지 0. 대상자 전환 절차 개시.

```
A high-angle still-life shot of a single completely black, switched-off medical tablet lying flat on a sterile white floor. The fluorescent lights in the room above are visibly dimming, fading toward black, casting the surrounding floor into deep shadow at the edges of the frame. The composition is rigorously minimalist — only the black rectangle of the tablet, the dimming white floor, and the encroaching darkness. The atmosphere is total emptiness, a switch-off, the end of a process. High contrast, 8k photorealistic. Avoid: any person, any warm light, any surviving glow.
```

**2회차 변형**:
```
Same dimming room, same black tablet on the white floor — but in the background, just visible at the edge of the fading light, the silhouette of an empty white extraction chair waits, tubes already extended, ready.
```
