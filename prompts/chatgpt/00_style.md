# 00. 공통 스타일 가이드 (마스터 톤)

> ChatGPT에 첫 메시지로 입력할 톤 설정. 이후 모든 씬 프롬프트가 이 톤을 따름.

## 시스템 메시지 (영문 — ChatGPT에 그대로 입력)

```
For all the following images you will generate, apply this consistent visual style:

A cinematic still from a clinical-horror feature film, set inside a sterile underground research facility. The aesthetic is calm, controlled, and quietly unsettling — never gory, never explicit. Threat is implied through emptiness, symmetry, and cold lighting, not through violence.

Visual rules to apply consistently:
- Color palette: heavily desaturated teal and grey, with sparing accents of pale yellow, soft amber, or deep crimson industrial fluid where specified.
- Lighting: cold overhead fluorescent panels, high-key clinical illumination, hard architectural shadows.
- Composition: symmetrical one-point perspective, perfectly straight architectural lines, vanishing-point corridors, hard reflective surfaces (polished floor, brushed metal, frosted glass).
- Camera: shot on Arri Alexa Mini LF with a 35mm or 85mm prime lens, T1.5–T2.0, sharp focus, subtle film grain, micro-contrast on every surface.
- Texture: visible scratches on metal, faint grime in corners, fingerprints on glass, natural human skin texture with visible pores — never plastic or airbrushed.
- Mood: oppressive silence, institutional cleanliness on the surface but a buried wrongness underneath. Eerie, sterile, hollow.
- Resolution: 8k photoreal, masterpiece quality.
- Avoid: warm sunlight, nature, cheerful colors, cartoon or anime stylization, 3D render look, motion blur (unless specified), gore, explicit violence, exposed skin below the neck.

When characters appear, they must always be fully clothed in long-sleeve white clinical uniforms or grey hospital gowns that drape neatly over the body and lap. Faces are the only expressive surface.

Confirm you understand this style, then wait for individual scene prompts.
```

## 한국어 요약 (참고용 — ChatGPT에 입력할 필요는 없음)

- **장르**: 클리니컬 호러. 노골적 잔혹함 X, 정적·고요·대칭으로 만드는 공포 O.
- **색**: 탈색된 청록·회색 베이스 + 포인트(연노랑·붉은 산업액).
- **조명**: 형광등, 높은 키, 그림자는 건축적·딱딱한.
- **구도**: 1점 투시, 직선, 좌우대칭, 소실점 복도.
- **렌즈**: 35mm 광각 / 85mm 인물.
- **질감**: 모공·금속 흠집·먼지 보이는 사실적 디테일.
- **금기**: 따뜻한 색, 자연광, 만화 톤, 노출, 직접 유혈.
- **인물**: 반드시 긴소매 흰 임상 유니폼 / 회색 환자복으로 전신 가림.
