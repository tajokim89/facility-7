## TITLE_NORMAL — 경비실 데스크 (정상)

```
A cinematic medium shot from a security guard's first-person perspective, looking down at a cold metallic desk inside a facility checkpoint. On the desk, arranged from left to right: one bulky old CRT monitor, switched off; one mechanical keyboard; one wired mouse; one stained white ceramic mug; and on the far right, a single vintage desk lamp with a warm amber bulb casting a small dim pool of light. Just behind the desk is a large observation window looking into a long, blindingly bright sterile facility hallway, with rows of repeating fluorescent ceiling lights receding into a vanishing point. Far down the hallway, a single distant figure in a white hazmat suit pushes a medical cart, tiny in the frame. The color palette is heavily desaturated cold teal and grey, broken only by the small amber pool of the lamp. Extreme textural detail: grime on the mug, fine scratches on the metal desk, a faint smudge on the window. Shot on Arri Alexa with a 35mm lens, high micro-contrast, masterpiece, 8k photorealistic clinical-horror aesthetic. Avoid: multiple monitors, modern flat screens, laptops, sunlight, any people inside the guard room itself, text, watermarks.
```

## TITLE_GLITCH — 경비실 데스크 (2회차 / 공포 변형)

```
The same security desk and the same observation window as the normal title shot, but heavily corrupted by digital glitch artifacts and data rot. The single CRT monitor is now alive, screaming with aggressive green static and the distorted silhouettes of human figures pressing against the glass from inside. The single desk lamp flickers violently in a deep red pulse, throwing the whole desk into intermittent crimson. Through the observation window, the hallway is now choked with thick red mist and long, slow-moving shadows; the figure that was pushing the cart is now a dark, glitching silhouette, frozen mid-step. Heavy chromatic aberration, scanlines, vertical tearing across the frame. The atmosphere is hostile, watching, wrong. Shot in the same 35mm composition, sharp focus on the corrupted objects, 8k photorealistic. Avoid: clean look, peaceful mood, bright steady light, warm cheerful colors, sunlight, text, watermarks.
```
